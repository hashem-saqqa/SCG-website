Our Services

Eleven service lines organised into two practices — Construction Services and Engineering Services — delivered under one point of contact.

▣

Construction Services

5 service lines

Delivery of the built work itself — from pre-construction through turnover — for residential, commercial, healthcare, and institutional clients.

C01

General Contracting (Residential & Commercial)

Managing and executing construction projects — coordinating labor, materials, subcontractors, schedule, and overall project delivery from start to completion.

C02

Interior Renovations & Tenant Fit-Outs

Modifying, upgrading, or building out interior spaces within an existing building to meet new functional, aesthetic, or tenant-specific requirements.

C03

Kitchen, Bathroom & Basement Renovations

Upgrading, reconfiguring, and modernizing interior spaces within an existing home to improve functionality, aesthetics, performance, and property value.

C04

Facility Upgrades & Modernization

Evaluation, improvement, and transformation of existing buildings to identify deficiencies, extend asset life, enhance performance, and align facilities with current operational, safety, and efficiency standards.

C05

Construction & Project Management

Planning, coordinating, and overseeing a construction project from pre-construction through completion to ensure it is delivered on time, within budget, and to quality, safety, and design requirements.

◈

Engineering Services

6 service lines

MEP design, commissioning, and building-performance work — the engineering discipline that makes the built work verifiable, efficient, and compliant.

E01

MEP Design, Coordination & Systems Integration

Designing, aligning, and connecting mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems so they function together efficiently, without conflicts, and in accordance with design intent and code requirements.

E02

Commissioning, Re-Commissioning, Retro-Commissioning, Building Envelope Commissioning & Quality Assurance

A systematic process of verifying that building systems are designed, installed, tested, and operate in accordance with project requirements, design intent, and applicable standards — to ensure reliable, efficient, high-quality performance.

E03

Energy Efficiency Improvements

Reduction of energy consumption while maintaining or improving performance, comfort, and functionality — typically through upgrades to systems, equipment, and building envelope components.

E04

Energy Efficiency Rebates & Tax Incentives

Managing financial programs offered by utilities, local and state governments, and federal agencies to offset the cost of energy-efficient upgrades, reducing upfront expenses and improving return on investment.

E05

Energy Auditing, Energy Retrofits & Facility Condition Assessments

Analyzing a building's energy use to identify inefficiencies, then implementing targeted retrofits to reduce consumption and operating costs. Facility condition assessments evaluate existing systems to determine end of useful life and weigh replacement against repair.

E06

Benchmarking, Energy Modeling, Measurement & Verification (M&V) and BEPS Compliance

Assessing current energy performance (benchmarking), predicting future performance and savings (modeling), and confirming actual results after implementation (M&V) — including BEPS compliance.

One team from pre-construction through verification.

Who we are

About Superior Construct Group

Superior Construct Group, LLC is a Maryland-based general contracting and engineering firm specializing in residential, commercial, healthcare, and institutional design and construction, with a unique focus on MEP design, systems integration, and performance-driven delivery.

Leveraging deep expertise in commissioning, energy systems, and building performance, we deliver projects that are not only built to specification — but operate efficiently, reliably, and as intended.

Seif Abaza, PE, CEM, CBCP

President & Chief Executive Officer
LinkedIn profile

Mr. Abaza manages all day-to-day operations of Superior Construct Group. He brings 28 years of experience in HVAC design, commissioning, retro-commissioning and re-commissioning, project management, and construction management.

His expertise spans ASHRAE Level I–III energy audits and multifamily inspections, central plant design, optimization, retrofit and O&M, energy modeling, LEED administration, energy retrofits and facility condition assessments, sustainable development and energy master planning, equipment inventory and deficiency generation, cost estimating, quality control management, post-construction measurement and verification, and BMS/EMS troubleshooting.

He is a Licensed Professional Engineer, Certified Energy Manager, Green Building Engineer, LEED® Accredited Professional, CQM certified, Building Performance Institute Building Analyst, and AEE Certified Building Commissioning Professional. His project history ranges from commercial buildings and schools to multifamily residential, private residences, hospitals and healthcare, hotels, and government and federal facilities.
